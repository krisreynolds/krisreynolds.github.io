Just a list of the things I've worked on or am still working on.

Scoutbook

Scoutbook is a clean, ad-free directory of curated links and resources for UK Scout Association volunteers and youth leaders. Built using Markdown, TOML, JavaScript, and CSS via Zensical , it helps you find the good stuff fast!

DDDPrinted.com

DDDPrinted.com is a hobby gone too far! A selection of 3D printed items, including Christmas decorations, NFC tags, keyrings and much more with plenty of customisation options. Mostly aimed at the Scouting and Guiding communities, but there's something for everyone!
